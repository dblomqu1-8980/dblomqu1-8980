# Hi, I'm Dan Blomquist

I run **[Aegis AI Advisory](https://aegisaiadvisory.com)**, helping small businesses and professional firms put AI to work. That means finding where it actually saves time and money, building practical tools and automations, training teams to use them well, and setting up sensible guardrails along the way. AI governance, through my **CLEAR-AI** approach, is one part of that work.

Before Aegis I led global IT delivery at Ford Motor Company, so I build for how organizations actually work: approvals, audit trails, and plain-language results.

## What I'm building
- **[aegis-vendor-risk-reviewer](https://github.com/dblomqu1-8980/aegis-vendor-risk-reviewer)** reviews AI vendor documents against a rubric, cites its evidence, and requires a human decision with an audit trail.
- **[aegis-policy-qa](https://github.com/dblomqu1-8980/aegis-policy-qa)** answers questions about a firm's AI policy and shows the sourced passages behind every answer.
- **[WattWatch](https://wattwatch.app)** is a Michigan utility rate dashboard that tracks MPSC filings, deadlines, and rate history, with every figure sourced to the order it came from.
  - Includes a **[Michigan data center tracker](https://wattwatch.app/data-centers)** covering every large-load data center project in the state: power requested, serving utility, status, and what the Public Service Commission has approved.
- **[michigan-dashboards](https://github.com/dblomqu1-8980/michigan-dashboards)** powers [906dashboard.com](https://906dashboard.com) and [upnorthdashboard.com](https://upnorthdashboard.com), live travel dashboards for northern Michigan.

## Focus areas
